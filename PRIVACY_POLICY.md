# Privacy Policy for AI Gesture Controller

**Last Updated:** October 2026

AI Gesture Controller ("the Application", "we", "our") is dedicated to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy details how the Application handles data when you use our touchless presentation control software on Microsoft Windows.

---

### 1. Camera & Video Data Processing
- **Strictly Local On-Device Processing**: The Application requests access to your webcam solely to detect hand postures and gestures (such as Open Palm, Thumbs Up, Peace Sign, and Pointing) in real-time.
- **No Video Recording or Image Storage**: Live camera frames are processed entirely in volatile system memory (RAM) on your local computer using the open-source Google MediaPipe framework. **No photos, video streams, or biometric recordings are ever written to disk, saved, or archived.**
- **No Data Transmission**: The Application does **not** transmit, upload, or sync camera data, hand coordinates, or any other sensory information to any external server, cloud platform, or third party.

---

### 2. Personal Information
- The Application does not require account registration, login credentials, or personal identification.
- We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or payment information within the software.

---

### 3. File & Application Access
- **PowerPoint & Presentation Files**: When you present, the Application opens your designated PowerPoint presentation file locally (`.pptx`) and sends standard Windows keyboard navigation events (`F5`, `Right Arrow`, `Left Arrow`, `Escape`, `Alt+F4`).
- No presentation slides, notes, or business documents are read, uploaded, analyzed, or shared.

---

### 4. Internet Connectivity
- The Application functions **100% offline**. An internet connection is not required to operate the gesture controls or camera tracking.
- No background telemetry, analytics trackers, advertising identifiers, or user tracking SDKs are embedded in the software.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
Because the Application does not collect or transmit personal information of any kind, it is completely safe for audiences of all ages, including children under 13.

---

### 6. Updates & Inquiries
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact the project maintainer.
